# Onboarding override

- Remove the application header, sidebar, and mobile tab bar for all five setup steps.
- Keep the form to a 720px reading width on desktop and a 16px gutter on phones.
- Use graphite and warm white for nearly everything; mint marks current progress, selection, and the single forward action.
- Do not use decorative gradients, glows, sparkles, or oversized pills.
- Prefer simple rows and two-column choices over dashboard card grids.
- Preserve Back, explicit step position, 44px controls, visible focus, and editable final recommendations.
