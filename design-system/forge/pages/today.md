# Today override

Today should answer three questions in order: How am I approaching today? What is my next action? What changed because I acted?

Mobile order:

1. Compact greeting and date.
2. Daily status strip (capacity, completed count, campaign day).
3. Daily mission with 1-3 large quest rows and inline progress.
4. Effort feedback or adaptation, only when relevant.
5. Collapsible `All due today` list.
6. A compact `Keep growing` preview linking to Character/Journey.

Rules:

- No giant progress donut on phone; use a thin progress bar and concise count.
- Confetti is short, sparse, and never obscures controls or text.
- Completion is the dominant interaction; adjust/reflection controls are secondary.
- Hide long recommendation explanations behind an accessible details disclosure on phone.
- Desktop may use a two-column mission layout, but the left column remains the action list and the sidebar remains narrow.
- Secondary world widgets do not all render on Today. Present one compact growth preview and route to their dedicated pages.
