# Quests override

- A collapsed quest card shows identity, compact schedule and rewards, current evidence, and the completion action.
- Long schedules, intentions, descriptions, heatmaps, and secondary statistics use one `Details` disclosure.
- Collapsed mobile cards stay below 280px and every control remains at least 44px tall.
- Completion remains the only filled action; edit, undo, sorting, and detail controls stay visually secondary.
